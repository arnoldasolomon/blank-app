# Go-to-market: first 30 days

Budget: under ₹50k. Runway: under 3 months. The goal of the first 30 days is to find out whether MSMEs will pay,
not to grow.

## Why this problem (data as of Sep 2026)

- About ₹8.1 lakh crore is locked in delayed payments to MSMEs (Economic Survey 2025–26).
- 2,56,892 delayed-payment cases worth ₹55,244 crore were filed on MSME Samadhaan up to 31 Dec 2025; 52,744 cases
  (₹8,397 crore) were still waiting to be examined.
- All new cases have gone through the MSME ODR portal since 15 Oct 2025. The MSMED Amendment Bill 2026 (passed
  Aug 2026) adds online dispute resolution and requires courts to order 50% of an award paid to the supplier when a
  challenge is pending over six months.
- Section 43B(h) of the Income Tax Act disallows buyers' deductions for MSE payables beyond 45 days, so buyers
  have their own tax reason to pay.

## Riskiest assumptions, in order

1. **Owners will act despite fear of losing the buyer.** This is the main reason they don't file today. The
   escalation ladder and interest waiver exist to address it.
2. **They will pay ₹499–₹3,000 up front**, not just a percentage on success.
3. **You can reach enough owners** beyond your first 5 contacts.

## Days 1–7: 20 conversations, no selling

Start with your 5 MSME owners and ask each for 3 referrals. Ask, don't pitch:

1. "Who owes you money right now that's more than 45 days late? How much, roughly?"
2. "What have you done to get it back so far?"
3. "Why haven't you gone further?" (Listen for: relationship fear, don't know the law, no time, lawyer cost.)
4. "Did you know the law entitles you to about 16.5% compound interest on that?" (Watch the reaction.)
5. "If I sent you the exact interest figure and a reminder you can WhatsApp to the buyer today, would you
   pay ₹499 for it?"

**Pass:** at least 5 of 20 say yes to question 5 **and** pay when you ask.
**Fail:** they like it but won't pay. Then test a pure success fee before dropping the idea.

## Days 8–21: pilots

- Run the tool for every owner who paid. Send reminders together and track: sent → buyer replied → paid.
- Record days-to-payment and amount recovered. This is your sales proof.
- Find one advocate willing to review and sign legal notices for a fixed fee paid directly by the MSME.
  Bar Council rules restrict advocates sharing fees with non-advocates, so don't take a cut of their fee.

## Days 22–30: decide

| Signal | Decision |
|---|---|
| 5+ paid, at least one recovery | Keep going: add AI invoice reading and a Hindi template, ask for referrals |
| Paid but no recoveries | Buyers ignore reminders: push the notice and ODR stages harder |
| Nobody paid | Stop or pivot. Don't build more features. |
