How a stock gets picked

Every week the screener runs the same six steps over the whole universe. A stock only reaches your desk if it clears every step, and a long only goes on together with a short.

Rules

Values come straight from config.yaml. ? Unconfirmed marks a value the PM has not signed off yet.

Daily use

The rules produce a ranked list; you make the final call and place the orders on IG or eToro yourself.
